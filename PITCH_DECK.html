Real Money Flow — Sepolia Testnet — Min 0.001 Fixed + Faucet Help + Mock Mode

Contract PaymentEscrow.sol

  • initiatePayment(bytes32 assetId, address to, uint256 tokenAmount) payable — min 0.001 ETH, fixes dust, faucet 0.5 free
  • confirmDrunixTransfer(paymentId, drunixTransferId) — onlyRegistrar, links TXN-...
