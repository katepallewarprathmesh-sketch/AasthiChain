# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

- Welcoming, patient behaviour towards people of any background or level of experience.
- Accepting review comments without taking them personally, and giving them without making them personal.
- Keeping discussion on the technical point at hand.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else connected to the project.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere a person is representing
the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Reports are read by the maintainer only. A report will be acted on: outcomes range from a private
word to a permanent ban from the repository, in proportion to what happened.

The maintainer is subject to this document too. A report about the maintainer's own conduct can be
raised publicly in an issue if you prefer.
